## Product Categories

NovaCart primarily sells:

- Wireless headphones
- Bluetooth speakers
- Computer accessories
- Mobile accessories
- Smart-home devices
- Keyboards and mice
- Charging accessories

## NovaSound Pro Wireless Headphones

Product Code: NSPH-100

The NovaSound Pro Wireless Headphones are over-ear wireless headphones
designed for everyday music, video, and communication use.

Key features:

- Bluetooth connectivity
- Active noise cancellation
- Built-in microphone
- USB-C charging
- Up to 30 hours of battery life under standard conditions

## NovaSound Lite Earbuds

Product Code: NSEB-200

The NovaSound Lite Earbuds are compact wireless earbuds designed for
portable use.

Key features:

- Bluetooth connectivity
- Charging case
- Built-in microphones
- Touch controls
- USB-C charging

## NovaCharge 65W USB-C Charger

Product Code: NC65-300

The NovaCharge 65W charger is designed for compatible smartphones,
tablets, and laptops.

Key features:

- USB-C output
- Maximum power output of 65W
- Over-current protection
- Over-voltage protection

Customers should verify device compatibility before purchase.

## NovaKey Mechanical Keyboard

Product Code: NKMK-400

The NovaKey Mechanical Keyboard is a wired mechanical keyboard designed
for desktop computers.

Key features:

- Mechanical switches
- USB connectivity
- Full-size layout
- Replaceable keycaps

## NovaHub USB-C Dock

Product Code: NHUB-500

The NovaHub USB-C Dock provides additional connectivity for compatible
computers.

Available ports include:

- USB-C
- USB-A
- HDMI
- Ethernet

Compatibility depends on the customer's computer and operating system.
