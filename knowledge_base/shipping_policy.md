# NovaCart Shipping and Delivery Policy

Document Type: Policy
Category: Shipping
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-08-01

## Standard Delivery

Standard delivery generally takes 3–7 business days after shipment.

The estimated delivery date associated with an individual order is more
specific than the general delivery estimate.

## Order Processing

Before shipment, orders generally move through:

Processing → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped

Processing time may vary depending on product availability and order
volume.

## Shipment Tracking

Customers can track shipped orders using the tracking information
associated with the order.

The support system can retrieve the current delivery status when tracking
information is available.

## Out for Delivery

An order marked "Out for Delivery" has entered the final delivery stage.

Delivery may occur on the same day, but the exact delivery time depends
on the delivery provider.

## Delayed Delivery

If an order has passed its estimated delivery date, the issue should be
investigated using the order and delivery information.

Customers should provide the order ID when contacting support.

## Lost Shipment

A shipment suspected to be lost may require investigation by the delivery
provider.

The AI assistant should create or escalate a support ticket when
automatic information is insufficient.

## Address Changes

Address changes may be possible only before shipment.

Once an order has been shipped, the delivery address may not be changed
through the normal support process.

## Delivery Exceptions

Issues involving repeated failed delivery attempts, incorrect delivery,
lost packages, or disputed delivery should be escalated when they cannot
be resolved using available order information.
