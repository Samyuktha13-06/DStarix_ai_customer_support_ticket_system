# NovaCart Account Policy

Document Type: Policy
Category: Customer Accounts
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-08-01

## Account Information

Customers are responsible for keeping their account information accurate
and up to date.

Supported account information may include:

- Name
- Phone number
- Delivery addresses
- Communication preferences

## Password Issues

Customers who cannot access their account should use the available
password recovery process.

The AI assistant must never request or store a customer's password.

## Account Security

Customers should not share:

- Passwords
- One-time passwords
- Authentication codes
- Full payment credentials

The support system should never ask customers to disclose these secrets.

## Account Verification

Certain account changes may require identity verification.

If verification cannot be completed automatically, the request should be
escalated to human support.

## Suspicious Account Activity

Reports of unauthorized account access should be treated as security
sensitive.

The AI assistant should provide general security guidance and escalate
the issue when manual investigation is required.

## Multiple Accounts

Customers should use the account associated with their order when
requesting order-specific support.

The system may request additional information when multiple accounts or
orders create ambiguity.
