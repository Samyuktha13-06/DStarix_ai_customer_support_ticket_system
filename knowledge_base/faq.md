# NovaCart Frequently Asked Questions

Document Type: FAQ
Category: General Support
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-08-01

## What is NovaCart?

NovaCart is an online electronics retailer that sells consumer electronics,
computer accessories, mobile accessories, and selected smart-home products.

Customers can place orders through the NovaCart online store and receive
order updates through their registered account.

## How can I check my order status?

Customers can check their order status using their order ID through the
NovaCart support system.

The available order statuses are:

- Processing
- Confirmed
- Packed
- Shipped
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered
- Cancelled
- Failed

Customers contacting support should provide their order ID whenever
possible.

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery generally takes 3–7 business days after an order has
been shipped.

The estimated delivery date shown for an individual order takes priority
over the general delivery estimate.

## Can I cancel my order?

Orders can generally be cancelled before they enter the shipped stage.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.
Customers may instead need to request a return or refund after delivery,
subject to the refund policy.

## Can I get a refund?

Eligible products may be returned and refunded according to the NovaCart
Refund Policy.

Customers should review the refund eligibility requirements before
requesting a refund.

## My payment was deducted but my order failed. What should I do?

Customers should contact support with the order ID and payment information.

The support system can check the order and payment status separately.

If a payment was successfully captured but the corresponding order failed,
the issue should be investigated and may require escalation to the payment
support team.

## What payment methods does NovaCart accept?

NovaCart supports:

- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- UPI
- Net banking
- Supported digital wallets

Availability of individual payment methods may vary depending on the
customer's location and checkout configuration.

## How do I update my account information?

Customers can update supported account information from their account
settings.

Certain sensitive information may require identity verification or human
support.

## How can I contact a human support agent?

Customers may explicitly request human support.

The AI support system should escalate the conversation when the issue
requires manual investigation or when the customer requests a human agent.

## What should I do if I received a damaged product?

Customers should report the issue as soon as possible and provide the
order ID and relevant details.

A damaged-product complaint may require additional verification and can
be escalated to human support.

## What should I do if I received the wrong product?

Customers should provide the order ID and describe the product received.

The issue may require manual investigation before a replacement or refund
can be approved.

## What happens if the AI assistant cannot resolve my issue?

The AI assistant should not repeatedly provide the same unsuccessful
response.

If the issue cannot be resolved, the system should create or update a
support ticket and escalate the issue to human support when appropriate.
