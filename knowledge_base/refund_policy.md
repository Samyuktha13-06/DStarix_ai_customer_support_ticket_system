# NovaCart Refund Policy

Document Type: Policy
Category: Refunds
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-08-01

## Overview

NovaCart provides refunds for eligible purchases subject to the conditions
described in this policy.

Refund eligibility depends on the product condition, purchase status,
return window, and reason for the request.

## Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 7 calendar days of delivery for
eligible products.

The product should generally be:

- Unused
- In its original condition
- Returned with applicable accessories
- Returned with available original packaging
- Accompanied by valid order information

Some products may have additional restrictions.

## Non-Eligible Situations

A refund may be rejected when:

- The return window has expired.
- The product shows significant signs of misuse.
- Required accessories are missing.
- The product has been damaged after delivery due to customer misuse.
- The purchase cannot be verified.
- The product belongs to a category excluded from return eligibility.

## Damaged Products

Customers who receive a damaged product should report the issue as soon
as possible.

The support team may request photographs, delivery information, and other
evidence before approving a refund or replacement.

## Wrong Product Delivered

If the customer receives a product different from the product ordered,
the issue should be reported with the order ID.

NovaCart may investigate the fulfillment record before approving a
replacement or refund.

## Refund Processing Time

After an approved refund is initiated, the amount is normally returned
through the original payment method.

The time required for the amount to appear in the customer's account may
depend on the payment provider or bank.

NovaCart cannot guarantee the exact posting time controlled by an external
financial institution.

## Failed Orders With Successful Payments

If an order fails but the payment was successfully captured, the payment
must be verified against the order record.

If the payment cannot be automatically reconciled with the order, the
case should be escalated for manual investigation.

## Refund Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require human review.

The AI assistant must not promise a refund when eligibility cannot be
confirmed.

## Important Support Rule

When a customer asks whether a particular order qualifies for a refund,
the AI assistant should first retrieve the relevant policy information
and, when necessary, use order information before making a recommendation.
